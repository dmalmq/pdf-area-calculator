# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Facility planning staff who repeatedly annotate floor-plan PDFs, organize facilities and stores by floor, prepare focused detail pages, and export area reports. They work with dense source material and need the interface to keep context visible while they edit.

## Product Purpose

Turn floor-plan PDFs into precise, readable facility-area reports without requiring a separate CAD workflow. Success means users can identify the current facility and floor at a glance, manipulate annotations confidently, and export reports whose structure and labels remain unambiguous.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, dependable. The interface should feel like quiet professional equipment: clear enough to learn without explanation and restrained enough to stay out of the work.

## Anti-references

Cramped and cryptic tooling: tiny controls, truncated names, ambiguous rows, hover-only information, and context that disappears while the user works. Also avoid decorative consumer-app styling that competes with the plans.

## Design Principles

- Keep facility, floor, and editing context visible at the point of work.
- Prefer explicit labels and familiar controls over compact but ambiguous affordances.
- Preserve precision while reducing the visual effort required to scan dense lists.
- Make direct manipulation reversible, predictable, and keyboard-accessible.
- Use restrained emphasis so active state and primary actions remain obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, text contrast of at least 4.5:1, non-color state cues, sufficiently large interaction targets, and reduced-motion support where motion is introduced.
